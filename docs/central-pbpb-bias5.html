Fortran fix and validation

Where the cascade first became nonfinite

With NJOB 2600421, the first bad momentum arose in DOKINEMATICS during the first coherent transfer in the failing collision call. The centre-of-mass fallback set recoil momentum squared p42 to zero. Recomputing the equal projectile momentum squared as E4new² − m42 gave −1.4510723636004724 × 10⁻¹⁸ GeV². The square root at line 6090 made the x momentum of outgoing projectile line 280 nonfinite. The incoming projectile line 226 was finite. Later transfers propagated it into projectile line 290 and its vertex and time fields.

All 13 Fortran medium shards used the reshuffle fix with the discard guard disabled. Each shard passed the HepMC ledger and finite-momentum checks.
