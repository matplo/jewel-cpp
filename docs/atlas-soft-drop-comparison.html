Thermal subtraction and sample definition

JEWEL hadronizes the scattered recoils and stores their incoming thermal momenta separately. After hadronization, constituent subtraction removes the incoming thermal transverse momentum and mass contribution from nearby hadrons with a maximum distance ΔR = 0.4. Leftover thermal entries are excluded before jet clustering. The corrected constituents feed both anti-kt and Soft Drop. JEWEL constituent-subtraction method

The 158–315 GeV samples use a 100–700 GeV pThat window. The 315–501 GeV samples use a separate 150–1000 GeV window. Each window has matched pp, medium and control samples with power-5 bias, ISR with MPI disabled, CT14nlo proton PDFs and a 30 ps particle lifetime selection. Jet constituents exclude muons and neutrinos. The Pb–Pb impact parameters are sampled proportional to Ncoll. The minimum-bias class overlap is 23.147 mb⁻¹ and cancels in the model RAA. Each hard window is normalized independently. The weighted pThat tails refer to the subtracted jets in the interval shown for each sample. Failed evolutions retain their generated weight and contribute zero jets.
