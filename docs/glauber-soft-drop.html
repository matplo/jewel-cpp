Soft Drop in central Pb–Pb

0–10% centrality at 5.02 TeV · Glauber-weighted quenched and zero-interaction samples versus pp

Model result and ATLAS reference

At 60–80 GeV, the widest tagged bin (0.2 ≤ rg < 0.4) gives RAA = 1.48 ± 0.34. At 80–100 GeV, the same bin gives 0.48 ± 0.15 in the quenched sample and 0.49 ± 0.14 in the zero-interaction control. The pilot does not establish a stable angular ordering across the jet pT slices.

Observable definitions

Cambridge/Aachen declustering finds the first split with z > 0.2 (β = 0). Its transverse momentum sharing is zg = min(pT1, pT2)/(pT1 + pT2). Its rapidity–azimuth distance is rg and θg = rg/0.4. The shape ratios use separately normalized pp and Pb–Pb densities for tagged jets with rg < 0.4. The rg-dependent RAA uses absolute cross sections and yields, including a separate untagged category.

The generator hard-scale limit is 100 GeV. These reconstructed jet intervals use |η| < 2, while ATLAS measures pT > 158 GeV with |y| < 2.1. The comparison illustrates the observable and the published qualitative trend of stronger suppression for wider jets. The independent zero-interaction sample shows how large bin-to-bin fluctuations can be, especially for narrow and untagged jets. ATLAS published results
