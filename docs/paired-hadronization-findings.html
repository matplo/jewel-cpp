Pythia6 and Pythia8 on the same parton events

Hadronization and decays can account for a substantial part of the 8–12 GeV jet-mass deficit. The paired sample does not resolve shifts in the 6–8 GeV mass or 0.6–0.7 pTD bins.

Shared input and settings

Both hadronizers processed 12,499 identical outgoing parton records, with the same flavours, four-momenta and colour connections. The jet selection found 20,477 jets before hadronization, 17,526 after Pythia6 and 17,460 after Pythia8.

The Pythia8 profile translates JEWEL’s explicit Pythia6 Lund and flavour settings. The vector-meson probabilities become vector-to-pseudoscalar ratios. Other engine defaults and fragmentation and decay algorithms can still differ.
